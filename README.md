# Life's Moments — Thasos

Website prototype for the Life's Moments holiday rental on Thasos.

- `index.html` — the new version: real photos of the property, editorial titles, a "day at the house" scene that goes from morning to night as you scroll, an auto-sliding strip with photos of each of the 11 rooms and the apartment, a Thasos guide, and Google reviews at the bottom. One fixed colour style; the preview panel on the right lists 14 colour options with their hex codes. Photos per room go in `UNIT_PHOTOS` in the script.
- `admin.html` — preview of the owner's admin panel (desktop) with fake data: a "Αιτήματα" list of the requests waiting for an answer (most urgent first, with the time left and the guest's message; answered ones fold below). Requests are answered on the calendar: a waiting request shows there as a dashed outline in the room it would get, can be dragged up or down to choose another room (saved on the request; dropping it on another request or movable booking swaps their rooms when both fit), and clicking it, or a request in the list, or a push notification, opens the calendar on its dates with the request's bar grown to about twice its height and Accept / Decline buttons under its name), a board of the 12 units for today (each card shows the next 14 days as a colour strip; "Λεπτομέρειες" opens the room's panel: who is in now, nights and revenue this month, a 14-day calendar where a tap closes or reopens a night, next bookings, closed nights, past stays, "Κλείσιμο απόψε" and "Νέα κράτηση" for that room), booking requests with a 24-hour timer and room picking, a one-week calendar (drag a booking up or down to move the guest to another room with the same dates: only our own confirmed bookings, never Booking.com-synced ones; while dragging, the target room shows whether it is free, fits the guests or is taken), bookings, customers with their stay history and notes, a price calendar where you pick dates and set specific prices, and settings. From the extranet study it also has: price and minimum nights for the days you drag over on the room calendar (prices show under each date), Bookings grouped by year and then month in sections that open and close (counts, pending requests and confirmed revenue on each header; the current year and the upcoming month open by default, everything opens while searching; date filters fold behind an "Ημερομηνίες" button; "Ταξινόμηση κατά" orders the bookings inside each month by date or by name, remembered on each device), and CSV download. Any booking can be deleted for good from its panel ("Διαγραφή κράτησης", after a confirmation; no email goes to the guest), except stays that came in through the Booking.com calendar sync, which would come back on the next sync and disappear by themselves when cancelled on Booking. The list shows only money that comes through us (site and direct): Booking.com stays are listed, with no amount or commission, a Messages inbox with quick replies (only guests who booked on the site; Booking guests are answered on Booking). Conversations live in `request_messages`: the message a guest writes in the booking form is the first one, owner replies are saved there and emailed to the guest by the `guest-message` Edge Function, and opening a conversation marks it read for both owners. "Νέα κράτηση" (Bookings, Calendar, or "Κράτηση εδώ" after dragging over free days of one room) lets the owner add phone or direct bookings: paid (any amount, discounts shown, cash/card/bank, goes to the accountant file), free stays for friends or own use (take the room, no revenue, left out of the accountant file), or tests (left out of everything). The accountant export in Settings now downloads a real CSV of the paid stays. The admin opens with a sign-in screen for the two owners; the signed-in owner shows in the sidebar, and accepting, declining, cancelling or adding a booking records who did it. Sign-in goes through one `AUTH` object in the script: today it uses two preview accounts (`idioktitis.a@example.com` / `idioktitis.b@example.com`, password `demo1234`), which protect nothing because the page is public. For the real thing, swap that object for Supabase Auth and lock the data with Row Level Security. Prices here are for the site only; Booking prices are set in the extranet. The site starts with the approval version: every request waits for an owner, the Booking.com calendar is read again the moment an owner presses accept, and a request nobody answers in 24 hours expires on its own (the hold is released). The Beds24 version is kept ready: open `admin.html?mode=auto` and the switch at the bottom left appears to flip between the two.

Push notifications: the admin can be installed on the phone (`admin.webmanifest`, icons in `icons/`) and uses `sw.js` to show notifications; tapping one opens that request (`admin.html?req=<id>`). In Settings each owner turns notifications on per device and can send a test request that arrives 5 seconds later. On iPhone they work only after "Add to Home Screen" and opening the admin from the icon (iOS 16.4+). Today the page shows the notifications itself; when the backend exists, a Supabase Edge Function sends real Web Push (VAPID) when a guest sends a request and 4 hours before it expires.
- `photos/` — photos of the property, resized for the web
- `hero-1600.mp4`, `hero-960.mp4` — hero video for desktop and mobile
- `hero-poster.jpg` — still frame shown while the video loads

The drone video is a placeholder until real footage of the property is ready.

## Run it

Open `index.html` in a browser, or turn on GitHub Pages (Settings → Pages → branch `main`, folder `/`).

## Backend (Supabase project "Life's Moments")

The site and the admin now run on real data only. The fake guests, bookings, messages and past stays are gone from the live admin (they stay only in `admin.html?demo=1`).

- **Real dates.** The site calendar and the admin use today's date in Greece. Past days are locked, and at midnight the calendar moves on by itself, even if the page stays open.
- **Availability on the site.** `index.html` and `mobile-test.html` read `site_availability` (only how many rooms are taken per night, never names) and refresh every 5 minutes and whenever the page comes back into view. A night counts as taken by: pending or confirmed site requests, stays the owners entered, and nights an owner closed.
- **Stays entered by the owners** (`owner_stays`): "Νέα κράτηση" saves paid stays (amount required when the dates have no price) and free stays (friends, family, own use); the guest's full name is required. Booking.com reservations are not typed in here: they arrive through the calendar sync, and nights can be closed by hand on the calendar. Cancelling, moving a guest to another room and closing or opening nights are all saved and show up for the other owner live.

- **Site → request.** `index.html` and `mobile-test.html` send the request form to the `submit-request` function. It checks the input, refuses nights that are already held (pending or confirmed requests, owner stays, closed nights and Booking.com reservations read from its calendars), saves the request in `booking_requests` and sends a push to every owner device. No card is held yet: Stripe comes later. The form also has an optional message to the owners (saved in `note`, shown as "Μήνυμα πελάτη" on the request in the admin) and an optional discount code. The code is checked as the guest types (`promo_check(code, email, check_in, check_out)`, public RPC): it works only with an email address the offer email was actually sent to (`promo_sends`), the offer is not cancelled, every night falls inside its stay dates and the book-by date has not passed; someone else's code is refused ("only with the email it was sent to"). It is checked and again by `submit-request`; a code that does not apply stops the request with a clear message. A valid code is saved as `promo_code` + `discount_pct`, the admin shows it on the request and takes the discount off the price. An offer email's link (`?promo=CODE`) fills the code in.
- **Admin sign-in.** Owners sign in with email and password (Supabase Auth). Only emails in the `owners` table can make an account ("Πρώτη φορά εδώ; Φτιάξε κωδικό"), and Row Level Security lets only them read or answer requests. To add an owner: `insert into public.owners (email, name) values ('...', '...');`. `admin.html?demo=1` still opens the old preview accounts with fake data.
- **Requests in the admin.** Real requests replace the demo ones on Today and in Bookings, update live, and accepting or declining saves to the database (if the other owner answered first, you are told).
- **Push.** Each owner turns notifications on per device in Settings; the device is saved in `push_subscriptions`. The server signs and encrypts the messages itself (`supabase/functions/_shared/webpush.ts`); the key pair is made on first use and kept in Vault. "Στείλε δοκιμή" asks the server to push to your own devices.
- **Every 10 minutes** (pg_cron → `request-reminders`): a reminder 4 hours before a request expires, and requests nobody answered within 24 hours are closed as expired.
- Code: `supabase/migrations/` (tables, rules, schedule) and `supabase/functions/` (`submit-request`, `push`, `request-reminders`, `send-email`).
- **Emails to guests** (Resend). Everything is automatic and the wording is fixed in `supabase/functions/send-email/templates.ts` (Greek and English, one look; owners do not edit the text). Six emails: *request received* ("we reply within 24 hours", sent when a request arrives), *confirmed* (dates, check-in/out times, address, map, how to get there, "good to know"), *before arrival* (ideas for Thasos; an hourly job sends it `prearrival_days` days ahead, 10:00-20:00 Athens, for bookings confirmed at least 12 hours earlier), *cancelled* (a confirmed booking that is cancelled: "we will find a solution together" and, only if `price_paid` is filled in, the refund amount), *rejected* and *expired*. A database trigger on `booking_requests` (`queue_guest_email`) calls the `send-email` function; failed sends are retried hourly for 48 hours (max 5 tries). The language is decided automatically (`detect_guest_lang` trigger). Payment and refund boxes appear only when `price_total` / `price_paid` are filled in; nothing is invented. The property details (times, address, map, directions, extra info, Thasos ideas, days before arrival, phone, sender) live in the single `email_settings` row; there is no admin screen for them any more, so they are changed in the database. Tables: `email_settings`, `email_log` (the old `email_templates` table is no longer used). Needs the Vault secret `resend_api_key` (or Edge Function secret `RESEND_API_KEY`); to reach real guests, a domain must be verified in Resend and the sender set in the same settings. Test requests (`is_test`) never get emails.
- **Offer emails.** An offer is one row in `promo_offers` (discount %, optional code, stay dates, book-by date, `send_at`); there is no admin screen for it any more. A job (`lm-promo-offers`, every 10 minutes) sends it once, 30 emails per run (Resend free plan: 100 a day). It goes only to guests whose latest confirmed, non-test stay is over and who ticked the optional "I would like to hear about offers" box in the request form (`booking_requests.marketing_consent`), and who have not unsubscribed. Every offer email has a signed unsubscribe link (HMAC, `unsubscribe` function, page `unsubscribe.html`) and `List-Unsubscribe` headers; opt-outs live in `marketing_optouts`. Offers and per-guest results are in `promo_offers` and `promo_sends`; a code in the offer link (`?promo=CODE`) is saved into the request note for the owners. Each request also gets a reference number (`ref_no`, shown as LM-1001...). Jobs: `lm-email-scan` (hourly) and `lm-promo-offers` (every 10 minutes); migration `20261014_08_guest_emails_v2.sql`.
- **Booking.com calendar sync** (iCal, one link each way per room; Settings → Booking.com). Each room (`ical_feeds`, 11 rooms + apartment) holds the link Booking.com gives under Rates & Availability → Sync calendars ("Export"); the `ical-sync` function reads every linked room every 15 minutes (pg_cron `lm-ical-sync`), when an owner saves a link, and on "Έλεγχος τώρα". Each Booking reservation becomes an `owner_stays` row (`source = 'booking'`, `created_by = 'Booking.com'`, `ical_uid`), so it closes the room on the site and shows on the board; it cannot be moved or cancelled here. A reservation that disappears from Booking's calendar is marked cancelled; dates that Booking only echoes back from us are skipped; a Booking reservation landing on nights already taken here sends a push ("Πιθανή διπλή κράτηση"). The other way, `ical/<token>.ics` (public, random token per room) lists the nights taken here (site bookings with that room, typed-in direct stays, closed nights, and pending apartment requests) for Booking's "Import". Booking's iCal only works when each room is its own room type on Booking.com; Booking reads our link on its own schedule (it can take a few hours).
- **Closed nights** (`unit_blocks`): closing or reopening nights on the admin calendar (one click, or drag over several days and rooms) is saved at once, so the site, the Booking.com export and the other owner see it. Past nights cannot be closed. The calendar always starts today.
- **Requests on/off** (Settings → Σάιτ → "Δεχόμαστε αιτήματα"): `site_settings.accepting`. When off, `submit-request` answers `closed` and the site shows a short note instead of the form (`site_open()` RPC). Requests already waiting are not touched.
